Press ? to open a keyboard shortcuts reference: a read-only overlay that
lists the shortcuts the app wires, grouped by where they answer (the
folder pane, the list, the composer, the palette, and more). Press ? again
or Esc to close it. It reuses the shortcuts list in Settings, so the two
never drift, and it stays out of the way while you type in a field.
