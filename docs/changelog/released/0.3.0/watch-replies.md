Astra Mail can now watch a conversation for its first reply. The row menu's
"Watch replies" entry watches the selected conversation (a single row; a batch
disables with its reason), and flips to "Unwatch" once it stands watched. When
the first reply lands, you get a "Reply received" notification naming the
reply, and the watch clears itself (one-shot); the base new-mail notification
still fires beside it. The reply notice fires even while the window is focused,
and a General settings switch ("Watched conversation replies", on by default)
turns the feature off. Watches live on this device only: they never move mail,
do not sync, and are not shared across devices. The watch survives the engine
re-keying a conversation when a reply's Message-ID sorts first, so the first
reply is caught reliably over plain IMAP.
