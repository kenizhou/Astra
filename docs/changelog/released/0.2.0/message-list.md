Opening a folder now shows its mail: sender, subject, and time on each row, with
unread and flag marks and a thread count when the list is grouped by conversation.
The list starts at one window of rows and grows as you reach its end, and the
toolbar switches between the flat list and conversation grouping. Marking a row
read or unread and setting or clearing its flag act on the row itself, take effect
at once, and hold across a refresh.

Platforms: desktop
