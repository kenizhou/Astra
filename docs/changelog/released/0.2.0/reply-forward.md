The reading pane answers mail. Reply, Reply all, and Forward open the composer
already filled in: the recipients a reply goes to, the Re: or Fwd: subject, and
the original quoted below with who sent it and when. A reply sends threaded, so
it joins the conversation it answers in every mail client; a forward carries the
conversation's chain to whoever you name. Both ride the same durable outbox as
a new message, so a send the app does not survive is neither lost nor sent
twice, and the sender's copy is filed in Sent as before.
