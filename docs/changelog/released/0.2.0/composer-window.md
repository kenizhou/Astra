Writing a message now happens in its own window: New Email, Reply, Reply
all, and Forward open a frameless pop-out composer with the subject centred
in its title bar, a formatting toolbar (bold, italic, underline, lists,
links, undo and redo) above the body, and a status bar with the save state
and a live word count below it. Several compositions can stand open at
once, each in its own window.

For a quick answer the reading pane gains a reply strip at its bottom: one
click on Reply docks the composer right under the message, prefilled and
threaded like any reply, and sending or discarding folds it away again.
Drafts are still kept as you type, and launching the app reopens the latest
stored draft in the strip.

Platforms: desktop
