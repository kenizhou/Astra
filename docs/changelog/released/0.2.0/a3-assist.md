Astra Mail now remembers, lends your own words back, finishes your
sentences, and translates what you read. Knowledge extraction reads an
account's most recent mail (at most thirty messages) and lands durable
entries, facts, preferences, projects, contacts, and persona notes
alike, each with the message it came from; the newest ten, persona and
preferences first, ride every reply draft as labelled data, and Forget
is the one exit, so nothing disappears on its own. The composer gains a
snippet library: your reusable texts, searchable in plain language (a
ranking call with a substring fallback), inserted at the cursor exactly
as typed. While you write, a dimmed suggestion may appear under the
current line two seconds after you stop typing; it never inserts
without your Tab or click, and dismissing it keeps it away for the
session. In the reading pane, a Translate action renders the open
message into English, Chinese, German, French, Japanese, or Spanish,
cached per language and body so the same translation never pays twice.
