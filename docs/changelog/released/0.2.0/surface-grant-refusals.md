A Microsoft account whose grant needs attention now says so on the
accounts page. An account whose calendar permission went missing (consent
narrowed or revoked at the provider) raises a reconnect banner naming the
address, and its folder pane group carries a calendar badge; both point
at the accounts page, where one tap re-runs the sign-in targeted at that
account and re-requests the full permission list. A sign-in that expired
or was withdrawn renders its own row stating exactly that, never a
server-outage badge, with the same one-tap sign-in action. Both states
are standing facts carried on the settings snapshot, so they survive
restarts and reappear until the fresh sign-in clears them.
