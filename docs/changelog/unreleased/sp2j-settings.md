Settings arrive. A Settings entry in the title strip opens the screen: a
category list beside a detail panel, carrying General (appearance following
the host or pinned light or dark, a 12 or 24 hour clock), Calendar (the
display zone, the first day of the week the grids and new repeat rules
anchor to, the horizon the diary opens with, the default calendar new
events land in), Reading (flat or threaded grouping), Composing (whether
the quoted original sits below or above the reply), and About (the version
and the attributions). Every value persists at once and survives a
restart.

The Accounts page lists every account with the legs it dials and opens its
own editor: the CalDAV and CardDAV origins an account's calendar and
contacts ride, saved with their login, re-dialled on save without a
restart; a failed re-dial keeps the legs that stood and says so. Removing
an account is confirmed by naming its address and takes the stored config,
the credentials, and everything the account was showing with it.
