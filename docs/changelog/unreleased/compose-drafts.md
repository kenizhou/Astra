Writing a message now happens in a composer of its own: rich text with
bold, italic, underline, and links over To, Cc, Bcc, and Subject fields,
with Cc and Bcc tucked away until they carry a value.

What you type is kept as you go. After a pause the draft is stored on the
mail server, replacing the copy before it, and the composer shows whether
the words are saved or still on their way. Closing the composer keeps the
draft; discarding it removes the stored copy.

The words come back too: launching the app opens the account's latest
stored draft into the composer, and saving from there writes over the same
copy rather than beside it.
