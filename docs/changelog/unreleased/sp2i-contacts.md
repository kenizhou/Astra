People arrive. A People view joins the shell beside Mail and Calendar:
one unified list across your accounts' address books, sectioned A to Z with
the accented letters filed under their base, searched in the engine, each
row wearing a coloured monogram. A person filed in more than one account
is one row that says so, and their detail names every source under Also in
and tags each value with the card it came from. Contacts can be created
into a writable address book and edited card by card: the editor asks
which card when a person is filed twice, refuses a form that names
nothing to file a contact under, and states its own save status beside
the list.

The composer's recipient fields gain autosuggest with pills: completions
rank from your synced contacts and from the people you have written to,
finish as removable pills while the one being typed stays text, and the
suggestion list floats over the form without moving anything under it.
Avatars reach the People view and the suggestions, the monogram until an
address-book photo arrives, served through a validated command.

Accounts grow a contacts and calendar leg: a stored dav section dials
both at boot, so the calendar finally fills on the desktop for an account
that carries one.
