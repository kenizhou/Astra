The desktop shell takes on the new layout: Mail, Calendar, and People move
from the title bar's segmented switch to a left icon rail that also carries
the outbox, agent, and AI panel toggles, the search field becomes a centred
pill, and the mail panes become resizable cards on a tinted surface with
pill dividers. Narrow windows swap the folder pane for a slide-in drawer
from the rail. Folder rows gain an accent selection bar and real icons,
message rows an unread ribbon, and the reading pane a bolder header. The
calendar takes the same language: a segmented view switch, chevron
navigation, rounded event chips, and a primary now-line, and the people
list and every dialog follow with the same rows, pills, and card chrome.
