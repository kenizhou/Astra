Astra Mail now drafts replies while you read. Opening a message quietly
arms one background generation: the thread's recent history, your
account's writing-style note (inferred from your recent Sent mail, at
most once a day), and the message itself, untrusted-wrapped, go to the
draft model, and the answer lands in a draft pool with its confidence
and one correlation id in the AI ledger. The reading pane surfaces at
most two of them as smart-reply chips, and only when the confidence
clears a floor and a cheap second-opinion judge agrees the reply is
genuinely useful; anything less stays pooled, queryable, and silent.
Accepting a chip opens the composer as a reply with the draft already
typed above the quoted original; you edit and send, through the outbox
as ever, and dismissing a message's chips hides them for good. Beside
the chips, a Summarise button answers with a cached-or-fresh summary of
exactly the body in front of you: the same body summarises once, and a
changed body summarises again. The style profile rebuilds itself on
demand from Sent mail; there is deliberately no model call anywhere in
the CLI (the one spelling that would need one refuses and points here).
